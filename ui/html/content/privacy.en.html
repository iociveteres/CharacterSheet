Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026.

For anything about your data, write to iociveteres@gmail.com.

What we keep and why

  • Your account: email address, username and password. The password is stored only as a hash. The email is used to log in, confirm sign-up and reset the password. When you change your username, the former one is kept with the date of the change: the last 10 are public on your forum page and shown to you on the account page. They cannot be hidden: they let others recognise you after a change of name.
  • What you create: character sheets, rooms, chat messages and rolls, dice presets, bestiary, portraits, avatar, forum posts and your forum "About". Sheets and chat are seen by those you give access to and by the members of the room. Forum posts, your avatar and "About" are public.
  • Technical data: your IP address and the page you request go into the server's logs. We use them to investigate errors and fight abuse. Counters of login and sign-up attempts per IP stay in the server's memory for a few minutes and are never written down.
  • Visit statistics: Umami counts page views. It sets no cookies and does not keep your IP. It sees the page, where the visitor came from, the browser, system, device type and country.

Who receives data

  • Umami: the visit statistics described above.
  • Plunk: sends the site's emails. It receives your email address and the text of the email.
  • Pictures added to forum posts by a link are loaded by your browser from the site that hosts them, and that site sees your IP.

Cookies and browser storage

Every cookie of the site is needed for it to work; none track you or serve ads, so the site does not ask for consent to them.

  • session: keeps you logged in, 30 days.
  • csrf_token: protects forms against forged requests.
  • lang, theme, theme_hue, tz, tz_ts: language, theme, colour and time zone, one year.
  • forum_density, forum_folded, forum_images, forum_mod_mode: how the forum looks, one year.
  • incident_notice_closed: that you closed the notice, one year.

In the browser's storage (localStorage) the site keeps interface settings: open panels, collapsed blocks, dice settings, where the initiative window is. They stay on your device and are not sent to the server.

How long data is kept

  • Your account, former usernames and what you created: as long as the account exists.
  • An unconfirmed account: 30 days after sign-up, then it is deleted.
  • An email confirmation link works for 3 days, a password reset link for 4 hours, email change links for 24 hours, a link to the account deletion page for 1 hour.
  • Your former email address after a change of email: 7 days. It receives a link to change the address back if it was not you who changed it, and the address is kept with that link. While the link works, no other account can take the address.
  • The archive of your data: 24 hours after it is built.
  • Forum notifications: 90 days.
  • Server logs: a few weeks at most, then they are overwritten.
  • Database backups: up to 30 days. Deleted data leaves them as the backups expire.

Your rights

You can find out what data of yours we have, get a copy of it, correct it or delete your account.

  • Get your data: "Download my data" on the account page. The site builds a ZIP archive: your account with all your former usernames, avatar, rooms, your sheets and portraits, bestiary collections, chat messages, forum topics, posts and "About", and the bans given to you. Sheets and collections are in the site's own format, so they can be uploaded back. A new archive can be built once every 30 minutes.
  • Delete your account: a button on the account page. We email you a link to the deletion page, where you confirm it. The account is deleted 3 days later, with a reminder a day before. Until then you can cancel it by the link in the email or by logging in; after that you cannot.
  • The username, email, password and avatar are changed on the account page. Changing the email takes your password and confirmation by links sent to the old and the new address. If you no longer have access to the old address, write to iociveteres@gmail.com and we will change the email by hand.

What stays after an account is deleted

Your email, username, former usernames, password and everything only you used are deleted. So that nothing breaks for others:

  • Your chat messages and forum posts stay, signed "Deleted user" instead of your name. On the deletion page you can tick boxes to delete them: the messages are deleted and the text of the posts is erased.
  • Rooms with other people go to the member you choose, your sheets in other people's rooms go to their gamemaster. Rooms where you are alone are deleted.
  • A public bestiary collection that others subscribe to stays without the author's name, unless you tick its deletion on the deletion page. Copies of your creatures that others already made stay.
  • Your name stays where other people wrote it: in quotes in their posts and in the source line of copied creatures.
  • Forum moderators' records of bans and deletions stay, with "Deleted user" in place of the name.

Deleted data stays in backups for up to 30 days.
